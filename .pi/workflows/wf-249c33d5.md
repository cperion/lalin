## Relationships

- **The forbidden primitive escapes (`any`/`table`/`table_ty`/`map`) are blocked at the DSL layer** (`dsl.lua:26-31`, `dsl.lua:110-112`). So no schema file declares them; the surviving smells are the higher-order structural ones the guide also lists (optional soup, context bags, string discriminators, catch-all variants, side-table substitutes, source bloat).
- **`interned`/`variant_unique` is the identity mechanism** and is used pervasively (720 `interned` product markers, 1386 `variant_unique` variant markers). Identity/interning is expressed *in the schema*, not as external node-keyed caches.
